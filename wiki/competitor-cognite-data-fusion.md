# Cognite Data Fusion Competitive Profile

## Externally verifiable facts

Cognite describes Cognite Data Fusion (CDF) as an industrial DataOps platform. Its official material emphasizes connecting operational, IT, and engineering data; contextualizing it into industrial models and knowledge graphs; governing access; and making the result available to applications through platform services, APIs, and SDKs. Cognite’s documentation is the authority for current services and deployment options.

## Pricing and implementation costs

The ingested pricing email prompted this update. Its figures were checked against the January 2026 Forrester study commissioned by Cognite. **These are modeled costs for a composite organization, not a current price list or customer quote.** Pricing varies with sites, tag volume, data types, and services. [Study, pages 6 and 12](https://cdn.sanity.io/files/p48fjve6/dev/8b9d78a6042b9871c63fccf21e5961d847c2e705.pdf#page=12).

### Subscription assumptions

| Model year | Sites deployed | Annual subscription before risk adjustment | Annual subscription after 10% risk adjustment |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Year 1 — Quick Start | 2 | $175,000 | $192,500 |
| Year 2 | 5 | $1,200,000 | $1,320,000 |
| Year 3 | 10 | $2,000,000 | $2,200,000 |
| Three-year total | | $3,375,000 | $3,712,500 |

The model assumes 30,000–50,000 tags per site. Dividing Year 3's $2 million by 10 sites gives an indicative $200,000 per site annually before risk adjustment; this is a calculated benchmark. The email's $3.71 million total includes the 10% adjustment, whereas its annual figures exclude it. [Subscription calculation, page 12](https://cdn.sanity.io/files/p48fjve6/dev/8b9d78a6042b9871c63fccf21e5961d847c2e705.pdf#page=12).

### Total cost and implementation effort

| Cost category | Risk-adjusted total, including initial costs | Present value at 10% discount rate |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Subscription | $3,712,500 | $2,918,802 |
| Implementation and project management | $3,861,529 | $3,400,956 |
| Combined | $7,574,029 | $6,319,758 |

Implementation includes internal labor, training, and external services; it is not all vendor services revenue. Assumptions include six staff for nine months initially, then four staff at 75% allocation, with one or two external consultants. Professional service fees are $650,000 initially and $400,000 in each of Years 2 and 3. These components are already included in the totals. [Cost model, pages 12–13](https://cdn.sanity.io/files/p48fjve6/dev/8b9d78a6042b9871c63fccf21e5961d847c2e705.pdf#page=13).

### Deployment and ROI context

The scenario expands from two sites to five to ten over three years. This rollout assumption does not establish a minimum deployment duration. The advertised 465% ROI combines customer interviews with composite-model assumptions; it is not a measured return for every customer. [Study, pages 2, 6 and 14](https://cdn.sanity.io/files/p48fjve6/dev/8b9d78a6042b9871c63fccf21e5961d847c2e705.pdf#page=14), [Cognite's study summary](https://www.cognite.com/en/forrester-total-economic-impact).

## Transpara interpretation — first-party positioning

CDF is most relevant when a buyer frames the problem as enterprise industrial-data contextualization and application enablement. Transpara can enter the same conversation when the required outcome is a governed operational view across sources, but Transpara’s first-party emphasis is KPI-centric operational understanding, visualization, alerts, calculations, and customer-controlled deployment.

Use the cost model to structure discovery around subscription scope, internal staffing, integration effort, and rollout stages. Compare those categories against a scoped Transpara proposal before asserting a cost advantage.

## Discovery questions

- Is the buyer selecting a data foundation, an operational application, or both?
- Must data be centralized, or can governed live access satisfy the use case?
- Which contextual model, dashboard, alerting, analytics, and deployment outcomes are mandatory?
- What must work in restricted-network or air-gapped conditions?
- What are the quoted subscription and implementation costs at two, five, and ten sites, and which data services are included?
- Which internal staffing, training, partner fees, and rollout assumptions belong in the buyer's total cost?
