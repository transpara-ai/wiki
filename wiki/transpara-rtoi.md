# Real-Time Operational Intelligence at Transpara

Real-Time Operational Intelligence (RTOI) is Transpara’s product frame for turning live operational data into contextual, actionable understanding. It emphasizes the questions “How is it going?”, “What changed?”, and “What needs attention now?” rather than periodic reporting alone.

## Stable product principles

- Live and recent operational state matters alongside history.
- KPIs add limits, status, and organizational meaning to raw values.
- Multiple source systems should be usable together without a mandatory wholesale migration.
- Asset, hierarchy, and metadata context should be governed consistently.
- Visualization, analytics, alerting, and AI should consume the same governed context.

## Evidence boundary

RTOI is both a product vision and a capability map. The maintained mapping matrix connects vision capabilities to ADRs, repositories, findings, and deployment evidence. A capability appearing in the vision does not by itself prove it is available in a released build. Seven capabilities were still identified as vision-only in the engineering documentation reviewed for this page.

Use the following labels in derivative material:

- **Implemented** only with code, configuration, or observed-release evidence.
- **Normative** for an accepted architectural obligation.
- **Planned** for funded or tracked work without implementation evidence.
- **Vision** for desired capability without a committed delivery claim.
- **First-party positioning** for comparative interpretation authored by Transpara.
