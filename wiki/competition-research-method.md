# Competition Research Method

Competitive knowledge must be current, attributable, scoped, and safe to retain.

## Source order

1. Vendor documentation, product pages, release notes, legal terms, security material, and filings.
2. Direct, authorized vendor responses or demonstrations with date and scope.
3. Customer or prospect evidence retained in its authorized system and anonymized here.
4. Reputable secondary analysis, labeled as secondary.
5. Transpara interpretation, labeled **first-party positioning**.

Search snippets, model recollection, unsourced battlecards, and autonomous-agent findings are leads, not canonical facts.

## Required record

For each time-sensitive claim, retain the claim, exact source URL or authorized record, source type, `verified_at`, reviewer, `review_by`, scope, and any uncertainty. Use a maximum 90-day review horizon for capability, packaging, ownership, pricing, and operating-status claims unless a shorter horizon is warranted.

## Comparison protocol

Name the buying context; agree dimensions; distinguish current fact from roadmap; capture unknowns; give every vendor the same evidence standard; and avoid numeric scoring unless weights and evidence are reviewable. Vendor self-description is evidence of the claim being made, not independent proof of performance.

## Safety boundary

Do not auto-export AI-SDR investigations, email, call transcripts, CRM data, customer production content, personal data, confidential proposals, or credentials. Summarize only authorized, anonymized lessons. Human publication approval is required before anything in this space is made public.
