# AVEVA PI System Competitive Profile

## Externally verifiable facts

AVEVA presents PI System as infrastructure for collecting, storing, enriching, and visualizing operations data. AVEVA describes PI Vision as a browser-based, self-service visualization layer for real-time operations data with displays, trends, alarms, and integration with PI System data.

Official pages establish vendor-described capability, not the fit or outcome in a particular customer environment. Current licensing, packaging, deployment, and compatibility must be checked from the applicable AVEVA documentation or proposal.

## Transpara interpretation — first-party positioning

PI is often part of the existing environment rather than something Transpara must replace. Transpara’s integration story can be complementary: use historian data alongside other operational and business sources, add KPI status and organizational context, and deliver a consistent operational layer. Where PI Vision is considered as the end-user layer, discovery should compare the required KPI model, rollups, alerting, multi-source context, governance, device experience, and application maintainability.

Avoid generic “dashboard versus dashboard” claims. Compare a named workflow and architecture using [[competition-rtoi-comparison]].
