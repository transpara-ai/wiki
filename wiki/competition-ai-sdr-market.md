# AI-SDR Market Context

AI-SDR products automate portions of prospect identification, research, personalization, outreach, response handling, qualification, and handoff. The category overlaps with sales-engagement, enrichment, CRM, dialer, and agent platforms.

## Transpara relevance

The `ai-sdr` repository is engineering input about Transpara’s own workflow architecture. It is not an authority for external competitor facts, commercial packaging, or product readiness. Its described elements include task orchestration, research and scoring, outreach integrations, human handoff, APIs, and long-running workers; explicitly planned sections must remain labeled planned.

## Evaluation dimensions

- data sources, identity resolution, enrichment provenance, and freshness;
- targeting, scoring, personalization, and approval controls;
- channels and provider dependencies;
- response interpretation, escalation, opt-out, and do-not-contact enforcement;
- CRM state ownership, deduplication, idempotency, and auditability;
- security, tenancy, retention, deletion, and model-provider boundaries;
- measurable funnel outcomes with a defensible baseline.

Vendor outcome claims are hypotheses until independently validated in Transpara’s own governed trial. See [[competitor-11x]], [[competitor-artisan]], and [[competition-research-method]].
