# Transpara Competition Overview

Competition is contextual: a company can compete for the same budget or outcome without being a feature-for-feature substitute. This space begins with two contexts already important to Transpara: real-time operational intelligence (RTOI) and AI sales-development automation (AI-SDR).

## Market contexts

| Context | Customer outcome | Representative alternatives in this seed set |
|---|---|---|
| Operational KPI and visualization | Understand current performance and exceptions | Visual KPI, Transpara Platform, AVEVA PI Vision |
| Industrial data infrastructure and context | Connect, organize, govern, and operationalize industrial data | Transpara Platform, Cognite Data Fusion, AVEVA PI System |
| Industrial analytics | Explore time-series behavior and publish analyses | Transpara tCalc and views, Seeq |
| AI-SDR | Identify, research, contact, and qualify prospects | Transpara’s internal AI-SDR work, 11x, Artisan |

These groupings are **Transpara first-party positioning**, not an independent analyst taxonomy.

## Evidence rules

Competitor facts must come from current primary sources and carry verification and review dates. A vendor claim proves what the vendor publicly claims, not independently measured efficacy. Comparisons must identify scope and unknowns. Pricing, ownership, availability, and security claims require direct current evidence; silence means unknown.
