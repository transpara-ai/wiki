# 11x Competitive Profile

## Externally verifiable facts

11x’s official site presents “Alice” as an AI sales-development worker and “Julian” as an AI phone agent. The site describes prospect identification, research, personalization, engagement, and qualification across multiple channels. These are vendor-described capabilities as of the verification date, not independently measured outcomes.

Current pricing, contractual terms, detailed integrations, geography, deliverability behavior, security controls, and data processing must be verified from current first-party documentation during an evaluation.

## Transpara interpretation — first-party positioning

11x is relevant where a buyer wants an externally supplied digital-worker experience spanning prospecting and multi-channel engagement. A governed comparison with Transpara’s AI-SDR work should focus on control boundaries, evidence provenance, workflow configurability, provider portability, escalation, CRM authority, deployment model, and inspectability—not on anthropomorphic agent names.
