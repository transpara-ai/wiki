# Artisan Competitive Profile

## Externally verifiable facts

Artisan’s official site presents “Ava” as an AI business-development representative. It describes lead discovery and enrichment, personalized email and social outreach, meeting generation, CRM synchronization, approval controls, and escalation or do-not-contact handling. These are vendor claims as of the verification date.

Current packaging, prices, supported integrations, calling availability, security scope, and data-processing terms require direct verification from current Artisan material or a governed vendor response.

## Transpara interpretation — first-party positioning

Artisan is relevant when a buyer prefers a packaged outbound workflow and unified sales workspace. A comparison with Transpara’s AI-SDR work should examine who controls the workflow and data, how research evidence is retained, where human approval occurs, which providers can be exchanged, how CRM authority is protected, and whether failure and opt-out behavior are auditable.

Customer stories are not a substitute for a controlled trial using Transpara’s baseline, audience, offer, and compliance constraints.
