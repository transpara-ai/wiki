# Competition Knowledge Base

Governed competitive and market intelligence for Transpara, beginning with
RTOI and AI-SDR contexts. The space separates externally verifiable facts from
Transpara's first-party positioning and records freshness expectations for
time-sensitive claims.

Prospect and win/loss evidence must be anonymized unless separately approved.
External-agent findings are research inputs, not canonical commercial claims.

Civilization space unless deliberately cross-placed.
