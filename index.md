# Transpara Knowledge Hub

One canonical knowledge graph, projected into distinct spaces for distinct
reader purposes. Articles keep stable identities while stewardship,
classification, and placement determine where they are discoverable.

## Knowledge spaces

- [Civilization](civilization/index.html) — philosophy, institutional architecture, history, research, and operational learning.
- [Transpara Platform](platform/index.html) — accurate commercial and technical product knowledge.
- [Competition](competition/index.html) — governed competitive, market, positioning, and win/loss intelligence.
- [DevOps](devops/index.html) — infrastructure, networking, containers, delivery automation, and operational runbooks.

The Knowledge Hub is advisory. Source repositories and approved source systems
remain authoritative for code, release state, delivery status, and runtime
truth. Customer production content is outside this repository's knowledge
boundary.
